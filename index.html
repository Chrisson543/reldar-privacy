Reldar Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Reldar is an iPhone app that tracks release dates of movies, TV shows, games and music and reminds you before they come out. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and what control you have over it.

What data we collect

When you sign in with Apple, we receive:

  • Your email address. If you choose "Hide My Email", this is an Apple relay address, not your real one.
  • Your name, if you choose to share it. Apple only shares it the first time you sign in.
  • An Apple account identifier that is unique to Reldar, so we can recognise you when you sign in again.

While you use the app, we store:

  • Your reminders: the title, its category, poster image link, release date, and the date and time your reminder is set for. For TV shows we also store the show's home country, so notifications can say which country's time an air date follows.
  • Your settings: notification choice, default reminder timing, whether to add events to your calendar, theme, which categories you follow, and your country or region. Your region is filled in automatically from your phone's settings and you can change it in Settings. We use it to show release dates for your country.

When you search, the words you type are sent to our server so it can fetch results (see "Services we use"). We do not link search words to your profile or keep a search history on our servers. Your list of recent searches is stored only on your phone and is removed when you sign out.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising, no tracking across other apps or websites, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools.
  • No location, contacts, photos or payment information.
  • We do not sell your data or share it for advertising.

Calendar and notifications

If you turn on "Add to system calendar", Reldar asks for calendar access. iOS calls this "full access", because an app can only manage its own events and remove them later with that level of permission. Reldar uses it to create a calendar called Reldar and to add and remove its own release events there. It does not read, use or upload your other calendar events, and no calendar data is sent to us. Reminders are delivered as notifications created on your phone. You can turn either off at any time, and turning off the calendar option removes the events Reldar added.

Why we use your data

We use your data only to run the app: to sign you in, to keep your reminders and settings across sessions, and to show you release dates and reminders. The legal basis is performing the service you asked for (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Services we use

  • Apple for sign in.
  • Supabase for authentication and the database that holds your account, reminders and settings.
  • Render for the server that runs the app's backend. Like any web server, it may keep standard technical logs such as request times.
  • TMDB (movies and TV), IGDB (games) and Spotify (music) for title information. Search words and your region (a two-letter country code) are passed to them to produce results and local release dates. We do not send them your account details.

Some of these providers process data outside the European Union. Where this happens, it relies on safeguards such as the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.

How long we keep your data

We keep your data until you delete your account. After deletion, your reminders, settings and sign-in account are removed from our database. Backups may take a short time to clear.

Your rights and choices

  • Delete your account: open Settings in the app and choose Delete Account. This removes your account and all stored data.
  • Access, correction, export: email us and we will send you a copy of your data or correct it.
  • Other rights under GDPR: you may also object to or request restriction of processing, and you may complain to a data protection authority. In Bavaria this is the Bayerisches Landesamt für Datenschutzaufsicht (BayLDA).

Children

Reldar is not meant for children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and your sign-in token is stored in your phone's secure Keychain. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect to reduce the risk.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we will tell you in the app.
